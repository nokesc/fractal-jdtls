## Decisions for review

These were made under the governor's delegation, within §11's intent.

1. **The design is not edited.** T002.01's acceptance says "The design itself isn't edited by this
   task", and the run's ground rules ask for `[REVIEW HERE]` proposals. The task's more specific rule
   wins, so the proposals are listed in HTTP-FINDINGS for T002.02 instead of being written into §11.
2. **The connection is stdio swapped for in-heap pipes.** It's a mechanism §11 doesn't list. It keeps
   §11's two rules, which the other candidates don't, or need an agent change to:
   - the connection is never reachable from outside the JVM;
   - the install stays read-only.
3. **The token can't leave the JVM's environment.** The proposal has two options for Chris, (a) an
   inherited file descriptor or (b) keeping the environment variable.
4. **The spike writes its own LSP framing and JSON**, over the pinned Gson. The passthrough sends
   params unchanged, the traffic log records every message, and the answers to JDT LS are a few lines
   each. lsp4j's typed endpoint adds nothing here.
5. **Autobuild off, lightweight auto-refresh off, and the worktree as the refresh scope for queries.**
   Each is the stable choice, with its cost measured.
6. **Two items left for Chris:**
   - the silent broken import: whether the endpoint repairs it itself, and whether a task should find
     the cause;
   - `-Djava.import.generatesMetadataFilesAtProjectRoot=false` in HTTP mode, which is a JVM option §1
     leaves to the client.
